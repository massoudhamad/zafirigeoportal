Zanzibar Fisheries and Marine Resources Research Institute

ZAFIRI is dedicated to marine research, fisheries management, and conservation of Zanzibar's rich marine biodiversity. Our Marine Atlas provides comprehensive spatial data for sustainable ocean governance.

Our Mission

To conduct scientific research and provide evidence-based recommendations for sustainable management of Zanzibar's marine and fisheries resources, supporting both ecological conservation and the livelihoods of coastal communities.

Marine Atlas Features

  • Interactive mapping of Marine Protected Areas
  • Fisheries zones and fishing ground data
  • Coral reef health monitoring
  • Mangrove and seagrass extent mapping
  • Sea surface temperature visualization
  • Bathymetric data layers

Key Research Areas

  • Fisheries stock assessment and monitoring
  • Marine biodiversity surveys
  • Climate change impact studies
  • Aquaculture development research
  • Ecosystem-based management

Our Partners

ZAFIRI collaborates with national and international organizations including:

  • Institute of Marine Sciences (IMS)
  • Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO)
  • Indian Ocean Tuna Commission (IOTC)
  • World Wildlife Fund (WWF)
  • Blue Ventures Conservation
